## Platform

## Product Purpose
An offline-first personal money manager with encrypted, optional sync. Record transactions, understand balances, and manage spending.

## Users
Inferred from the existing application: individuals managing personal accounts and budgets on desktop and mobile.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing Angular 22 / Ionic 9 PWA with an Android wrapper. IndexedDB is the source of truth. Preserve vault encryption, currency handling, accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, recurring rules, reports, import/export and sync. Never fabricate financial activity to fill an empty screen.

## Brand Commitments
Easy Docket. User requested an Apple-inspired frontend redesign with impeccable guidance and authorized implementation and running the app.

README.md, existing application routes, functional unit and browser tests, and local icon assets. Audience detail beyond personal finance remains inferred.
